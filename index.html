FROM MY FINAL TRAINING DIARY

Small tasks. Useful engineering lessons.

Laser cutting machine maintenance

Assisted with identifying damaged hoses, measuring replacement lengths and checking the machine after replacement. This reinforced the link between fault identification, replacement specifications and operational checks.

Three-jaw chuck reverse engineering

Measured a lathe chuck and prepared sketches and SolidWorks modelling work. The exercise connected physical measurement with part geometry and assembly understanding.

Precision measurement and lathe fundamentals

Practised using measuring instruments and studied the centre lathe, chuck, carriage and cutting tools. The final diary records caliper and micrometer work alongside basic turning and thread-cutting learning.
